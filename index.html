Official Business Site

Charles Anthony Paris (born April 30, 1980), professionally established as Paris Eighty / OTG, is a prominent American business executive, multimedia producer, and technical architect. He is the founder and CEO of Over Time Grind Productions, LLC and Paris Eighty (P80), a multi-faceted enterprise spanning commercial print, media, and digital software development. Through his Over Time Grind framework, Paris has engineered high-level digital strategies for major industry figures, including Beanie Sigel, and composed for broadcast networks such as BET. His current digital footprint reaches millions of impressions monthly, supported by a verified network of over 200,000 professional followers.

Professional History & Strategy

Beginning his professional tenure in 2002, Paris transitioned to the Philadelphia market in 2011 to formalize Over Time Grind Productions. His operational background includes high-stakes mentorship from industry pioneers Bruce Hawes and MC Lyte. In 2023, he scaled his operations under the Paris Eighty (P80) brand, centralizing his technical development tools, commercial print logistics, and independent music publishing into a single high-efficiency legacy platform.
